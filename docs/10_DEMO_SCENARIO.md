# Demo Scenario

## Objective

Demonstrate the complete closed loop:

Feedback
→ AI
→ Evidence
→ Recommendation
→ Human Action
→ Outcome

## Step 1 — Student

Open the feedback screen.

Current meal:
Dinner

Tap:
2/5

Select:
Too cold

Record:
"Aaj rice bahut thanda tha."

Attach a photo.

Submit.

Target:
under 10 seconds for the core interaction.

## Step 2 — AI

Show:

Language
Sentiment
Theme
Severity
Confidence/evidence

## Step 3 — Duplicate Shield

Attempt a repeated submission from the same demo student for the same meal.

Show safe duplicate handling.

## Step 4 — Manager Dashboard

Show:

Temperature complaints
Historical baseline
Change

## Step 5 — Evidence

Open "Why?"

Show:
- related feedback
- time concentration
- meal concentration
- historical comparison

## Step 6 — Recommendation

Show AI recommendation.

## Step 7 — Human Decision

Manager creates an action.

## Step 8 — Outcome

Show before/after metrics.

Use cautious wording:

"The issue appears to be improving."

## Step 9 — Community

Show students discussing the issue.

Use AI to summarize the discussion.

## Step 10 — Admin

Show:
- student control
- feedback review
- media review
- moderation
- audit trail

## Final Message

Students speak.
AI understands.
Admins act.
Mess improves.
